# Lesson Plan: 2026-11-06

## Goals

- Understand how the class runs: sessions, transcripts, and extraction prompts.
- Understand the knowledge capture workflow this team is building.
- Start work on your own extraction prompt.

## Before class

- Read [SAGE: Saving All Gathered Expertise](https://isocpp.org/files/papers/P4046R0.pdf).
- Read [How an Agent Harness Works](../tutorials/tutorial-agent-harnesses.md).

## Schedule

Times are flexible. Try this layout, keep what works, and adjust what does not.

| Block                      | Time (PT)             | Length | What happens                                                                                          |
|----------------------------|-----------------------|--------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Session 1                  | 9:30–10:20 a.m.       | 50 min | Instructor teaches, tells stories, or answers questions. The session is transcribed.                  |
| Individual study and lunch | 10:20 a.m.–12:30 p.m. |        | Add the transcript to the repo, run your extraction prompt, and work on your own prompt. Take lunch whenever it suits you. |
| Session 2                  | 12:30–1:20 p.m.       | 50 min | Second teaching session, built on the questions from study time.                                      |
| Break                      | 1:20–1:30 p.m.        | 10 min | The instructor leaves.                                                                                |
| Team breakout              | 1:30–2:00 p.m.        | 30 min | The group reviews the output and writes the questions for the next session, without the instructor.  |

## Additional information

- **Shared work.** There are no individual assignments. The class has one shared assignment, and people sign up for the work themselves.
- **Transcript processing.** Adding each transcript to the repo and running the extraction prompts is a rotating job. The team decides the rotation.
- **Cursor.** Everyone runs their prompts in Cursor so results can be compared.
- **Prompts by hand.** You can ask AI questions, but write the prompt prose and Lua by hand.
